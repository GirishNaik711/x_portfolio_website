02 STRATEGIC ARCHITECTURE & SYSTEM DESIGN

CabLoot

REVOLUTIONIZING RIDE-SHARING ACCOUNTABILITY

CabLoot is a satirical ride-sharing accountability platform designed to combat the chaos of last-mile travel. Built with ClaudeCode & Gemini CLI, Supabase, and a touch of irreverent humor, it transforms everyday ride-sharing frustrations into public accountability metrics. Users can submit stories of late pickups, wrong turns, or awkward driver encounters, which are then immortalized in a global leaderboard of "Ride Shame."

  • Edge Compute: Leveraged Vercel Edge Functions for global low-latency routing and real-time API responses without cold-start delays.
  • Serverless Database: Architected a resilient Supabase schema with Row Level Security (RLS) policies and triggers to handle real-time data ingestion and user-generated content moderation at scale.
  • Community-Driven Moderation: Implemented a transparent "Upvote/Downvote" system for ride submissions, allowing the community to moderate content while maintaining a satirical context.
  • Geospatial Storytelling: Leveraged Supabase GeoPoint and PostGIS to map user-submitted incidents, creating a "Ride Shame Heatmap" that visualizes the most problematic areas for ride-sharing across the city.
  • Real-time Story Ticker: Integrated a streaming "Ride Ticker" that displays anonymized, real-time incidents across the globe, creating a sense of global community and shared ride-sharing woe.

Vichaar: The Thought-to-Video Engine

Multi-Tool Agentic Orchestrator

An autonomous content refinery that transforms raw Slack inputs into polished LinkedIn assets and AI-generated video. This system utilizes a stateful, event-driven architecture where two independent n8n workflows synchronize through a shared Redis persistence layer to manage complex, multi-day human-in-the-loop interactions.

  • State-Machine Orchestration: Uses a "Session" concept in Redis to track content through seven distinct stages (from new to done), ensuring context is never lost across asynchronous API calls.
  • Recursive Feedback Loops: Features a dedicated "Revision Sub-Pipeline" that allows real-time refinement of LinkedIn posts and video scripts before final rendering.
  • Cross-Platform Synthesis: Integrates Perplexity for real-time research, Claude 3.5 Sonnet for creative writing, and HeyGen for high-fidelity avatar synthesis.
  • Fail-Safe Interactions: Implements a "Redis Refresh" pattern to bypass the data-loss limitations of standard n8n Wait nodes, maintaining 100% data integrity during long-running video renders.

12 Applied AI Products

Financial Guardian

Scheduled Market Briefings on Slack

Watches an Indian-market portfolio (Zerodha Kite equities, mutual funds, bonds and a Google Sheets watchlist) and posts a morning briefing, an end-of-day wrap and urgent alerts to Slack.

  • A morning briefing at 07:30 IST and an end-of-day wrap after the close, covering equities, mutual funds, bonds and a watchlist, with market context: indices, FII/DII flows, global data, sectors and the market regime
  • Each briefing is built in stages: a small Claude model analyses every holding in parallel, the results are assembled, and a larger model writes the final message with links to the news and filings it used
  • Urgent alerts during market hours for price drops, important filings, news keywords, trading halts and promoter pledge increases. Reacting with a check mark in Slack acknowledges one; unacknowledged alerts are listed in the next morning briefing
  • One long-running Python process runs every job from a single YAML schedule, skips NSE holidays, and keeps its state in PostgreSQL and Redis (Docker Compose)

13 Foundational ML

Student Score Predictor

End-to-End Regression Pipeline

A Flask app that predicts a student's math score from demographics, test prep and the reading and writing scores. Trained on a 1,000-row dataset.

  • A web form takes gender, ethnicity, parental education, lunch type, test prep course and the reading and writing scores, and returns the predicted math score
  • Preprocessing is a scikit-learn ColumnTransformer: median imputation and scaling for the scores, most-frequent imputation and one-hot encoding for the categories. It is saved so predictions use the same transform as training
  • Training compares seven regressors (Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, XGBoost, CatBoost, AdaBoost, Decision Tree, Linear Regression) with grid search on an 80/20 split and keeps the best by test R² (0.88 on the last run)
  • Split into ingestion, transformation and training components with separate train and predict pipelines, logging and custom exceptions
